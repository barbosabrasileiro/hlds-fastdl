Forsaken-Hostages Rescue
Original zg_forsaken by South Korea Nexon
Port by Izuko Fukuda

In a city that has long been in ruins due to the war, it has become a utopia 
for a group of local gangsters: they hide four kidnapped national politicians in two 
abandoned buildings in the abandoned city, but the disappearance of the dignitaries 
undoubtedly attracts the attention of the country's military: they are soon in the vicinity 
of the deserted city, and the terrorists have already prepared their defenses and are 
threatening the lives of politicians as leverage! Counter-terrorist forces to fall back, a 
huge ransom and a fleet of helicopters to escort them out of the country.

Counter-Terrorists:
Raid the abandoned city where the terrorists who have kidnapped 
four of the country's most important political figures are hiding, find and rescue 
the hostages and lead them back to the rescue site, or eliminate all 
the terrorists in the city.
Tip:If your squad have enough members,you may want to split up:
one team uses smoke grenades and sniper rifles to delay the terrorists,
while the other team goes around to rescue the hostages.

Terrorists:
The main force of the lowly government lackeys has been 
scared off by the bargaining chip of being a hostage and the 
fleet of helicopters that are being prepared to honorarium 
and escort you to a life of ease abroad, but they are still t
rying to send in the HRT to rescue the dignitaries to put a kibosh 
on your plans to defend the buildings in the abandoned city 
and the hostages and to eliminate the Counter-Terrorists.
Tip:When leaving at least one person to guard the HRZ and
another to guard the hostages,make good use of radio comm
unication to check in with each other in case things change.

Other Notes:
There are four hostages in the abandoned city.
