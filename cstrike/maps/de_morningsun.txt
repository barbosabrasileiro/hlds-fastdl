De_MorningSun: A morning sunrise
By: hamst3r- greenbak@velocity.net

Textures by: The dornerking and
the talanted people at countermap

Story:

A terrorist faction has been sent out
to destroy a factory which, was obtained
by the U.S. government after a chemical
weapons raid. The terrorists must destroy
the factory, covering up any evidence that 
they produced chemical weapons and that
the faction even exists.

Terrorists: Bomb one of the two weakest
parts of the factory, causing all evidence
to be destroyed.

Counter-Terrorists: Secure the factory of
all hostiles, and protect the weak points.
